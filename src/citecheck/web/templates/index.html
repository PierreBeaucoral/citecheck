What it does

  • Retraction check. Combines Crossref's update-to with OpenAlex's is_retracted flag. Together they catch ~67% more retractions than either source alone.
  • Fabrication detection. Five-signal rule-based detector. Articles: precision 0.779, recall 0.957 on a 770-reference labeled corpus.
  • Journal quality. Eight-signal continuous risk score combining DOAJ membership, Scopus indexing, h-index, citations per article, journal-flood pattern, APC, publisher portfolio size, and a hand-curated concern list.
  • Claim verification. RAG against the cited paper's full text. Precision 0.860, recall 0.977 on a 100-triple hand-labeled benchmark.

Methods + numbers are documented in the companion paper. Apache-2.0 OSS; reports are public-by-URL. No accounts; no tracking.
